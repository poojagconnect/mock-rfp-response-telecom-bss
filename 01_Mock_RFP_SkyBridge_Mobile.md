# Request for Proposal: Cloud BSS Transformation
**Issued by:** SkyBridge Mobile India (fictional operator)
This is a mock RFP I wrote for a learning exercise. All names, numbers and dates are made up.

## 1. Background
SkyBridge Mobile India is a mid-size telecom operator with about 12 million subscribers, around 80% prepaid. Its billing platform is 15 years old, and customer data sits in separate CRM tools for mobile, broadband and enterprise. Launching a new plan takes 8 to 10 weeks and needs code changes.

## 2. Objectives
- Replace the legacy billing and CRM stack with a cloud-based BSS.
- Launch new plans, bundles and promotions in days, not weeks.
- Launch 5G plans and enterprise IoT connectivity services.
- Give customers one self-service experience across all products.

## 3. Scope
In scope: CRM, product catalog, order management, charging, convergent billing, invoicing, self-service channels, migration of existing subscribers, integration with network and payment systems.
Out of scope: network upgrades, device sales, retail store systems.

## 4. Requirements
**Priority:** M = Mandatory, O = Optional

| ID | Area | Requirement | Priority |
|----|------|-------------|----------|
| R01 | CRM | Single customer view across mobile, broadband and enterprise accounts | M |
| R02 | CRM | Self-service app and web portal for plan changes, top-ups and bills | M |
| R03 | CRM | Case management and complaint tracking with SLA alerts | M |
| R04 | Catalog | One catalog for plans, bundles, add-ons and promotions | M |
| R05 | Catalog | Launch a new plan or bundle in days without code changes | M |
| R06 | Order Mgmt | Order orchestration from capture to activation, with failure handling | M |
| R07 | Charging | Real-time (online) charging for prepaid and data usage | M |
| R08 | Billing | Convergent billing for prepaid, postpaid and hybrid customers | M |
| R09 | Charging | Support for 5G network slicing and IoT usage-based plans | O |
| R10 | Billing | Tax handling and invoicing that meet Indian regulations | M |
| R11 | Billing | Dispute handling and bill adjustments | M |
| R12 | Platform | Cloud-native, deployable on public or private cloud | M |
| R13 | Platform | Open APIs aligned with TM Forum standards | M |
| R14 | Delivery | Migration plan for existing subscribers with minimal downtime | M |
| R15 | Platform | Data security, audit trails and role-based access | M |
| R16 | Delivery | Local support, training plan and 12-month rollout timeline | M |
