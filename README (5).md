# Mock RFP Response: Billing and CRM for a Telecom Operator

I made this project to learn how a presales team answers an RFP. I invented a telecom operator called SkyBridge Mobile India, wrote a short RFP for it, and then answered it the way a presales consultant would.

This is a learning exercise. The customer, the numbers and the dates are made up, and the answers describe a generic BSS product, not any real company's product. I used public reading and generative AI tools to help me research and draft, and I checked and edited everything myself.

## Files
- `01_Mock_RFP_SkyBridge_Mobile.md`: the RFP I wrote (16 requirements)
- `02_Compliance_Matrix.xlsx`: my answer to each requirement, plus a summary tab
- `03_Solution_Approach.pptx`: a short deck with the architecture and delivery plan
- `04_Executive_Summary_Assumptions_Risks.md`: summary, assumptions, risks and questions for the customer

## What I learned
- How CRM, product catalog, order management, charging and billing connect in one customer journey
- How to write a compliance matrix and say "partly met" when that is the honest answer
- Why assumptions and open questions matter as much as the answers

## Terms I had to learn
- CRM: customer records and support cases
- Product catalog: where plans, bundles and prices are defined once
- Order management: takes an order and makes sure it gets activated everywhere
- Charging: counting usage and deducting it (in real time for prepaid)
- Billing: turning usage and plan fees into a bill with tax
- BSS / OSS: business systems (customer, billing) vs network systems
- TM Forum: industry body that publishes standard telecom APIs and process models
